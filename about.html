Our story

VastraWave Surat — India ke textile hub — se curated ethnic wear laata hai. Har piece quality check ke baad dispatch hota hai, isliye aapko milta hai showroom jaisa finish, boutique ke bajay me nahi.

Shipping policy +
• Dispatch within 24 hours (working days).
• Delivery in 3–6 working days, pincode ke hisaab se. Metro cities me 2–4 din.
• Orders above ₹999 par shipping free; usse kam par ₹60 shipping charge.
• Tracking link SMS + WhatsApp par bheja jata hai.
• Shipping PAN India available hai.
Return / exchange policy +
• 7-day easy size exchange — product unused, unwashed aur tags ke saath ho to.
• Sizing issue par exchange free hai; hum reverse pickup arrange karenge.
• Damaged / wrong item milne par unboxing video ke saath 48 hours me report karein — full refund ya replacement.
• Refund 5–7 working days me original payment method par aa jata hai (COD par bank transfer).
• Custom-stitched ya sale items par exchange applicable nahi.
